New Node

March 28, 2026 · 2 min · VortexSpace

Hey there, builders. Big news from the VortexSpace Host.

We told you we were building something different, and today we’re proving it. The migration is complete, and the performance is better than ever.

NL1 to IT1: We’re home.#

The migration from our old NL1 (Netherlands) node to the brand new IT1 (Italy) node is officially successful.

VortexSpace is now officially running from its home base. Low latency, Italian power, and zero downtime during the switch. Italy is now on Top!

What this means for you:#

  • Lower Latency: Faster response times for your bots and sites.
  • Improved Stability: Brand new hardware dedicated to the free tier.
  • Local Power: Optimized routing for our core community.

Database Migration: 100% Complete#

We didn’t just move the files; we moved the heart of your projects. The database migration is officially finished. All MariaDB instances, users, and tables have been safely ported to the new infrastructure.

Your data is safe, your PhpMyAdmin is ready, and everything is running smoother than before.

Private NL2 Node is Online 🚀#

For our backend systems and specialized services, we are proud to announce that the Private NL2 Node is now fully online.

While the community thrives on IT1, our infrastructure is expanding across Europe to ensure VortexSpace remains the most reliable free hosting platform out there. No single point of failure. No compromises.

Zero Bullshit. Just Progress.#

We promised real resources and a reliable place to run your ideas. Moving to IT1 is just the first step in making VortexSpace the strongest hub for indie developers.

  • 24/7 Uptime: Confirmed.
  • Real CPU/RAM: Guaranteed.

Haven’t joined the migration yet?#

If you’re new here, now is the perfect time to jump in. The new node is fresh and ready for your code.
