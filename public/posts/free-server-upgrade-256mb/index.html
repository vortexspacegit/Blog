Free Server Upgrade

April 13, 2026 · 1 min · VortexSpace

We’ve just rolled out a major upgrade to our Free Server plan following the results of the community poll 🎉

What changed?#

Based on community feedback, Option A has been selected, bringing significant improvements to free resources:

  • 🧠 RAM: 128MB → 256MB
  • ⚡ CPU: 25% → 35%
  • 💾 Storage: 768MB → 1024MB

About the new system:#

Alongside the upgrade, we are introducing a server activity management system designed to optimize resources:

1. Server purge system#

A purge will only happen:

  • When necessary due to resource pressure, or
  • On a monthly scheduled check

2. Inactive server rules#

Servers will be marked inactive if:

  • No activity in the last 7 days
  • Activity includes: startup, file edits, console usage, or any interaction

3. Suspension & recovery#

  • Inactive servers are suspended for 24 hours
  • Users can open a support ticket to restore access
  • After 24 hours without action → server is permanently deleted

Thank you <3#

Thanks to everyone who voted and contributed to shaping this update.
This helps us keep VortexSpace fast, stable, and fair for everyone.
